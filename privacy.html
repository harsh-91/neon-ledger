Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Neon Ledger / Statement Importer · Created by Harsh Nair · Made in India.

The desktop app processes bank statements on your computer. It does not require a Neon Ledger account and does not automatically send your statements or transactions to us.

Information the app processes

When you select a statement, the app reads its file contents, bank and account identifiers, transaction details, dates, amounts, balances and any password you enter to unlock it. It uses these to recognize the statement, reconcile and deduplicate transactions, and store results in the PostgreSQL database you configure. Statement passwords are used for parsing and are not saved. Database connection settings are stored locally; on Windows, the saved database password is protected for your Windows user account.

If a file needs manual column mapping, an encrypted temporary copy may be kept on your device for up to 24 hours or until you finish or remove the mapping. Imports, generated backups, exports, settings and diagnostic reports remain in locations on your device that you control. Imported transactions remain in your database until you remove them or the database.

Network access and sharing

  • Core importing and database operations can work without internet access. The app does not include advertising or product-use telemetry.
  • On Microsoft Store installations, Microsoft manages app delivery and updates under its own terms and privacy practices. If you enable Windows backup to OneDrive, Windows may include app data in that backup; review your Windows backup settings. On legacy installations, an optional update check requests public release information from GitHub; a manual download contacts GitHub only when you request it.
  • Optional REST API and MCP services are disabled until you enable them. They listen on your computer's loopback address and require an app-generated key. An external program or AI client you choose to connect may process, store or transmit the data it reads according to that program's own practices. Review its privacy terms before connecting it.
  • The diagnostic tool creates a report on your device. It includes app and operating-system version information, setup checks and redacted setup events, but is designed to exclude statements, transactions, database contents, passwords, API keys, protected configuration values and PostgreSQL log contents. It does not send the report automatically. If you choose to email it to support, review the report, attach it and send it yourself; your email provider will process the message and attachment. We use support messages to investigate and reply; messages and attachments may remain in the support mailbox until manually removed or under the email provider's retention rules. You can request removal by emailing the contact below.

Security and control

The app's local web interface and optional API/MCP endpoints bind to 127.0.0.1 by default. API keys are stored as hashes, and you can disable services or revoke keys in the app. Other software running under your computer's security context may still access local resources, so protect your Windows account, database and backups. You control the statement files you select, the database connection, exports, backups, optional integrations and whether to send a support report. Uninstalling the app may not remove separate databases, exports, backups or emailed reports; review those locations if you want them deleted.

Website and third parties

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process visits and downloads under its privacy statement. Microsoft Store, GitHub, your email provider, a database server you choose, and third-party clients you connect are separate services with their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is a bank-statement utility, not designed for children. We do not knowingly request children's personal information through an account or cloud service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The effective date above will change when this page is revised. For privacy questions or requests about a diagnostic report you deliberately emailed to us, contact harshnair02@hotmail.com. For data stored only on your device or in your own database, you manage that data locally.
